<section class="home-section" id="about">
  <h2>About in brief</h2>

  <p class="section-lede">
    I am a physicist and data scientist with more than twenty years of experience
    in data analysis for the Virgo gravitational-wave experiment. My work focuses on
    extracting weak astrophysical signals from complex detector data, with particular
    attention to noise analysis, system identification, and machine learning techniques
    for detector characterization and signal detection.
  </p>
